Customer Value & Retention

High-Ticket Leverage & Spender Pareto

A small minority of high-basket orders and top-tier customers disproportionately underpins gross business revenue.

High-Value Orders (> ₹1,000) 52.2% Revenue Generated by only 15.3% (306 transactions) of total sales volume.
Top 5 VIP Concentration ₹1,48,470 Contributed by Customer IDs: #3, #1, #5, #2, and #4 across 155 unique shoppers.
  • ↗
    Basket Threshold Leverage: Orders exceeding ₹1,000 drive over half of gross turnover, demonstrating strong pricing tolerance.
  • ◎
    Concentration Risk: Top 5 VIPs alone drive 16.3% of total revenue. Losing even 2 top accounts materially damages margin.
Executive Action Formalize a Tiered VIP Loyalty Program with threshold benefits (e.g. Free delivery or priority perks on orders > ₹1,000).

Seasonality & Trends

Q4 Surge Dictates Annual Procurement

Historical performance reveals cyclical revenue expansion peaking sharply each year between October and December.

2022 Annual Peak ₹72,880 Achieved in December 2022, after steady rises in Oct (₹68.2K) & Nov (₹68.9K).
2023 Annual Peak ₹69,145 Achieved in December 2023, following strong September & October volumes.
  • ▲
    H2 Expansion: Revenue doubles between off-peak troughs in February/April and peak year-end holiday momentum.
  • ▼
    Q1/Q2 Moderation: Annual lows average around ₹16K–₹25K per month, demanding disciplined cost control.
Procurement Action Lock in inventory supplier contracts and logistics lead times by early Q3 to avoid stockouts in late Q4.
